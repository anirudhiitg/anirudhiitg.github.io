# Sthir Life — Marketing Plan (Get Orders)

_Rooted in Nature. Steady in Life._
Goal: turn strangers into repeat WhatsApp orders for **Amla Powder** and **Moringa Powder**, shipped pan-India.

---

## 1. The one-line strategy

Build trust with *honest, farm-first content*, send that audience to WhatsApp, convert with a
low-risk **starter bundle**, and keep them with a **monthly refill** habit. We are not selling a
supplement — we're selling a healthier relationship with food. Every post, caption, and reply
should feel calm, honest, and grounded (never hype, never fear).

## 2. Who we're selling to (priority order)

1. **Urban wellness families (30–50)** — want trustworthy food for the family. *Primary.*
2. **Conscious professionals (25–45)** — stress, energy, better daily habits.
3. **Parents** — healthy additions for kids' food.

Where they are: Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube Shorts, and parenting/wellness communities.

## 3. The funnel (what we're optimising)

Reach (content) → Follow / Save → **WhatsApp message** → First order (starter bundle) →
Reorder (refill) → Referral. WhatsApp is the checkout. Every channel's job is to start a chat.

---

## 4. Offers & pricing ladder (the conversion engine)

Set actual prices before launch; structure matters more than the numbers.

- **Try-me Starter Bundle** — Amla + Moringa (small packs) at an intro price + free/flat shipping.
  The single most important offer. Low risk = first order.
- **Everyday Refill** — standard pouch, the repeat-purchase workhorse.
- **Family Pack** — larger size, better per-gram value (raises order size).
- **Monthly Refill (subscription-lite)** — "reply REFILL and we'll message you every month."
  Runs entirely on WhatsApp; no software needed. This is where the real revenue compounds.
- **Referral** — "Share Sthir Life with a friend — you both get X off your next order."

First-order nudges: free shipping over ₹X, a handwritten farmer's note in the box, a QR code on
the pack linking to the product story (already built into the site's packaging philosophy).

## 5. Channels & tactics

### A. Instagram + YouTube Shorts (primary engine)
- Post 4–5x/week (mix of Reels + carousels). Reels drive reach; carousels build trust.
- Reuse the same video as a YouTube Short and WhatsApp Status — 1 shoot, 3 placements.
- Bio link → the website; pin a "How to order" highlight and a "Reviews" highlight.
- DM auto-reply: anyone who comments a keyword (e.g. "AMLA") gets a WhatsApp link.

### B. WhatsApp (the order + retention channel)
- Add the **WhatsApp Business app**: catalog (Amla, Moringa, bundles), quick replies, labels
  (New / Ordered / Repeat), and a greeting message.
- Use **Broadcast lists** (not spam) for a monthly "harvest note" + restock/offer.
- Post **Status** daily (farm moments, orders being packed) — social proof to existing contacts.

### C. Trust & social proof
- Collect a photo/gT review after every order (ask in the delivery follow-up).
- Show the farm, the hands, the small-batch process — authenticity is the differentiator.
- Feature "why handmade, not factory" repeatedly; it's the core objection-buster.

### D. Community & partnerships (low cost, high trust)
- Micro-influencers (5k–50k) in wellness/parenting/slow-living — gift the starter bundle,
  ask for an honest story. 5 small authentic creators > 1 big paid post.
- Get featured in wellness/parenting WhatsApp & Telegram communities and newsletters.
- List on **Google Business Profile** and relevant Indian marketplaces later (Phase 3).

### E. Light paid (only after organic proves the message)
- Boost the 2–3 Reels that already performed organically.
- Instagram/Facebook lead or click ads → WhatsApp, retarget site visitors.
- Start tiny (₹200–500/day), scale only what returns orders.

## 6. Content plan — 3 pillars (from the brand)

1. **Honest Food** — what's in the pack, how it's grown, benefits without hype, simple recipes
   (moringa in dal, amla in water). *Educational + appetite.*
2. **Connection to Nature** — farm footage, harvest, seasons, "where your food comes from."
   *Emotional trust.*
3. **Slow Living** — calm mornings, family rituals, why slower is better. *Brand world / aspiration.*

Weekly rhythm (example): Mon education · Tue farm/behind-the-scenes · Wed recipe ·
Thu review/testimonial · Fri slow-living reflection · weekend Status + engagement.

### Ready-to-use post ideas
- "3 ways to use Moringa powder this week" (Reel).
- "Why our Amla is sun-dried and stone-ground, not factory-made" (carousel).
- "A harvest morning on the farm" (Reel, ties to the website story).
- "What ₹__ of honest food looks like" — starter bundle unboxing.
- Customer review screenshot → carousel.

### Caption starter (tone reference)
> Modern life moves fast. Nature doesn't.
> Our Amla is picked by hand, sun-dried, and stone-ground in small batches — nothing added.
> If we wouldn't feed it to our family, we won't sell it to yours.
> 🌿 Reply "AMLA" or tap the link to order on WhatsApp.

Hashtags (rotate 8–12): #slowliving #honestfood #moringa #amla #farmtotable #cleaneating
#indianwellness #ayurveda #organicindia #mindfulliving #healthyfamily #farmfresh

## 7. 90-day launch roadmap

**Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2: Foundation**
- Finalise prices, packaging, shipping (courier + rates), and the starter bundle.
- Set up WhatsApp Business (catalog, greeting, quick replies) and the contact-form endpoint.
- Shoot a content bank: 10–15 Reels/photos in one or two farm sessions.
- Set up Instagram bio, highlights, and the site's Formspree endpoint.

**Phase 2 — Weeks 3–6: Launch & prove the message**
- Post daily for the first 2 weeks; run a launch offer on the starter bundle.
- Personally message your warm network (friends/family) — first 20–30 orders come from here.
- Gift bundles to 5–10 micro-influencers.
- Collect reviews from every single order; reshare them.

**Phase 3 — Weeks 7–12: Scale what works**
- Double down on the 2–3 content angles that drove the most WhatsApp chats.
- Launch the **Monthly Refill** to your first customers.
- Turn on small retargeting/boost budget behind proven Reels.
- Start a simple monthly "Harvest Note" WhatsApp broadcast + email to buyers.

## 8. Metrics to watch (weekly)

- WhatsApp chats started, and chat → order conversion %.
- First orders, reorder rate, average order value.
- Cost per order (once paid starts) — keep it well under your margin.
- Content: reach, saves, shares (saves/shares predict trust better than likes).
- North-star: **repeat customers per month**.

## 9. Guardrails (brand voice)

✅ Calm, clear, honest, simple. Show the farm and the process.
❌ No aggressive health claims, no fear marketing, no wellness jargon, no fake urgency.
Decision test: *"Does this help people feel more connected to nature, food, and a slower life?"*
If yes, do it. If not, don't.
