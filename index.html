Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our farm-grown products, shipping, and slow-living practices.

Our small family-run farm is located in Hanumangarh, Rajasthan. We grow our own amla and moringa trees using natural, organic practices without synthetic pesticides or chemicals.

Unlike factory brands, we don't use fully automated machines or synthetic fillers. We harvest by hand, sun-dry or shade-dry the produce carefully, and stone-grind in very small batches. This preserves the maximum possible nutrients and guarantees complete purity.

We believe in human-to-human interaction! Simply tap any "Order on WhatsApp" button on our website. It will open a WhatsApp chat with us pre-filled with your selected product. We’ll guide you through payment and shipping details directly in the chat.

Yes, we ship to all pin codes across India. Since we grind and package fresh in small batches, orders are dispatched within 24-48 hours. Depending on your location, it typically takes 3 to 6 business days to reach your doorstep.
